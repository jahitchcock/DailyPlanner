❦ Paper Planner Generator

In the print dialog: Paper size = A5, Margins = None, enable Background graphics.

✂ Cut & stack assembly

  1. Print all sheets double-sided (flip on long edge), keep them in order.
  2. Stack the whole printed pile in order, all facing the same way (sheet 1 on top).
  3. Cut the entire stack once, straight down the vertical center.
  4. Set the left pile on top of the right pile (keep each pile's order).
  5. The combined stack now reads in order — the blank leading page is your cover, so the month spread lands on pages 2–3.
